Written report excerpt

How findings and recommendations are written for leadership.

Sample deliverable. All data is synthetic and for illustration only.

Executive summary

Repeat purchases fell in the second half of the period while new-customer orders held steady. The drop is concentrated in one product category and one sales channel.

Key findings

  • Repeat orders in the affected category declined month over month.
  • The decline began after a change to delivery times in one channel.
  • Other categories and channels remained stable.

Recommendations

  • Review delivery performance in the affected channel first.
  • Track repeat rate by category on the monthly dashboard.
  • Re-run the analysis after the next full month to confirm the trend.

Method and limits

Figures come from the cleaned order data with duplicates removed. The analysis shows association, not proven cause, and the recommendations are framed accordingly.
